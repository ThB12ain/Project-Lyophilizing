Component washing that starts every batch clean.

Glass vials, stoppers and seals are washed with purified water and water for injection on validated cycles, then handed straight to sterilization.

Final rinse
WFI, 80 °C
Particulate spec
USP <788>
Throughput
up to 24,000 vials/h

Automated vial washing

Rotary washers with alternating WFI and compressed-air jets clean the inside and outside of every vial.

Stopper and seal processing

Rubber closures are washed, siliconized if required, and bagged for steam sterilization.

Endotoxin control

Wash cycles are qualified to remove endotoxin and particulates, with rinse water sampled each run.

Capabilities

Washing at a glance

Washing runs in-line with depyrogenation, so clean vials never wait in open air.

We qualify each new component format with spiked particulate and endotoxin challenge studies.
