Our story

Why we exist

Lyophilized products fail in predictable ways. A cycle runs a few degrees too warm. A formulation is moved to a new site without its data. A filled vial waits too long before it reaches the lyophilizer. Each of these problems sits at a handoff between teams or companies.

We built Sublyra Sciences to remove those handoffs. Washing, sterilizing, compounding, filling, lyophilization, capping and labeling all happen at one site, with formulation and synthesis scientists down the hall.

Good equipment matters, but programs succeed because of judgment and communication. We hire scientists who explain what they see, raise problems early and treat your product as if it were their own.
