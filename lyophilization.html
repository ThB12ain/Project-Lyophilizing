Services / Lyophilization

Lyophilization is what we do best.

We design cycles from your formulation's thermal data, scale them from pilot to production dryers, and run them under GMP with full process monitoring.

Thermal characterization

Lyomicroscopy and DSC find your collapse and glass transition temperatures before any cycle is written.

Cycle development and optimization

We build a recipe that keeps product temperature below collapse, then shorten primary drying without risking the cake.

Scale-up and GMP manufacturing

Pilot and production dryers share controls and sensors, so a cycle transfers with predictable results.

Capabilities

Lyophilization at a glance

Every dryer carries wireless product probes, capacitance and Pirani gauges, and pressure-rise testing to confirm the end of primary drying.

Controlled ice nucleation is available to make freezing uniform from vial to vial.
