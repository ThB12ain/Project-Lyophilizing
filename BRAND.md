# Sublyra Sciences brand

## Name

**Sublyra** comes from *sublimation*, the step in lyophilization where ice turns straight to vapor, and *lyo*, short for lyophilization.

## Logo

![Sublyra Sciences logo](assets/img/logo.svg)

The mark is an ice-crystal hexagon with one edge open. Three dots rise out of the opening like vapor leaving the vial, and the solid hexagon in the center is the dried cake left behind.

| File | Use |
|---|---|
| `assets/img/logo.svg` | Full logo on light backgrounds |
| `assets/img/logo-white.svg` | Full logo on dark backgrounds |
| `assets/img/logo-mark.svg` | Mark only, for social avatars and small spaces |
| `assets/img/favicon.svg` | Browser tab icon |

Keep clear space around the logo equal to the height of the mark's center hexagon. Don't recolor, stretch or add effects.
